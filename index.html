Transducer placement

Channel 1 (tone) uses the test ear's earphone and Channel 2 (masker) the other one, wherever they actually sit. An earphone off the patient or above the ear loses about 40 dB. A covered ear gains the BC occlusion effect, so keep the test ear uncovered for BC and cover the non-test ear with the masking earphone when masking.

Patient Setup

Hover over a row name for details. BC transmission loss worsens bone conduction reaching that cochlea without changing its cochlear threshold or AC, so ear-specific BC = cochlear threshold + BC transmission loss (use it for a Carhart notch; a negative value is a BC gain, as in a superior canal dehiscence; usually 0). See the Otosclerosis and SSCD presets for examples. The conductive rows do the opposite: they worsen AC to that cochlea and leave BC alone.

Measured values: Wallaert et al. (2024), Trends in Hearing, Table 7 (TDH 39 and inserts), up to 2 kHz.

Method Review

Reviews your technique from the presentation log: familiarisation, step sizes, ascending confirmation (2 responses on ascending presentations at the same level), frequency order, BC side, masking plateaus, tone length and gaps. It uses only what you did and saw, not the patient's thresholds; use Check for accuracy. Reset the log when you start a new patient. These are teaching guidelines: follow your tutor's protocol where it differs.

Masking Chart

Automatic draws the chart live from your presentations: each tone at each masker level (filled = response, open = no response) and, once confirmed (2 responses on ascending presentations), the threshold at that masker level. Manual shows only points you add with Plot masking (P). A plateau is thresholds within 5 dB over at least 20 dB of masker (15 dB is acceptable for a masking dilemma). Guide lines come from your plotted audiogram: start at the non-test ear's AC; over-masking may begin at the test ear's BC + the IAA of the masking rules chosen in Options. You can keep testing with this panel open. Session only.

Presentation Log

Every tone you present, with its settings at onset, how long you held it, the gap since the previous tone (ISI) and whether the response light came on. This log is kept for this session only: download the CSV before closing or reloading.
